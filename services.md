<section class="section-alt">
  <div class="section">

    <div class="service-block" id="consultation">
      <h2>Initial Fertility Consultation</h2>
      <p>Your journey begins with a comprehensive, one-to-one consultation. We take the time to understand your medical history, lifestyle and goals, review any previous investigations or treatments, and answer your questions honestly. You will leave with a clear, evidence-based understanding of your options and a personalised roadmap for the next steps.</p>
    </div>

    <div class="service-block" id="semen-analysis">
      <h2>Semen Analysis</h2>
      <p>A cornerstone of male fertility assessment, semen analysis evaluates sperm concentration, motility (movement) and morphology (shape), along with volume and other key parameters. The results provide essential insight into fertility potential and help guide the most appropriate treatment pathway.</p>
    </div>

    <div class="service-block" id="iui">
      <h2>IUI — Intrauterine Insemination</h2>
      <p>IUI is a gentle, minimally invasive fertility treatment in which specially prepared, high-quality sperm is placed directly into the uterus around the time of ovulation. It is often a first-line option for couples with mild fertility challenges, and can be combined with ovulation monitoring or stimulation.</p>
    </div>

    <div class="service-block" id="freezing">
      <h2>Egg, Sperm &amp; Embryo Freezing</h2>
      <p>Fertility preservation gives you the freedom to plan for the future. Using advanced vitrification techniques, we can safely freeze and store eggs, sperm or embryos — whether for medical reasons, ahead of treatment that may affect fertility, or simply to preserve your options for later in life.</p>
      <ul>
        <li><strong>Egg (oocyte) freezing</strong> — preserve unfertilised eggs.</li>
        <li><strong>Sperm freezing</strong> — secure a sample for future use.</li>
        <li><strong>Embryo freezing</strong> — store fertilised embryos for future transfer.</li>
      </ul>
    </div>

    <div class="service-block" id="pgt">
      <h2>PGT — Preimplantation Genetic Testing</h2>
      <p>PGT screens embryos for genetic and chromosomal conditions before transfer, helping to improve the chance of a healthy pregnancy. We support all types of preimplantation genetic testing:</p>
      <ul>
        <li><strong>PGT-A</strong> — screening for aneuploidy (abnormal chromosome number).</li>
        <li><strong>PGT-M</strong> — testing for specific inherited (monogenic) conditions.</li>
        <li><strong>PGT-SR</strong> — detection of structural chromosomal rearrangements.</li>
      </ul>
    </div>

    <div class="service-block" id="icsi">
      <h2>ICSI — Intracytoplasmic Sperm Injection</h2>
      <p>ICSI is an advanced laboratory technique in which a single healthy sperm is injected directly into an egg to assist fertilisation. It is particularly effective in cases of male factor infertility or when previous fertilisation attempts have been unsuccessful, giving many couples a renewed chance of success.</p>
    </div>

  </div>
