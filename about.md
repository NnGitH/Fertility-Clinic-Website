        <p class="team-role">Clinical Embryologist &amp; Scientist</p>

        <p>Nicholas Nicolaides is a Clinical Embryologist holding an <strong>MSc in Clinical Embryology from the University of Oxford</strong> and a <strong>BSc in Molecular Biology from Middlesex University</strong>. With extensive hands-on experience across every stage of assisted reproduction — from semen analysis and sperm cryopreservation through to ICSI, embryo biopsy for PGT, and blastocyst vitrification — Nicholas brings both scientific rigour and compassionate patient care to the fertility laboratory.</p>

        <p>His career spans multiple clinical settings, including the prestigious <strong>Guy's and St. Thomas' NHS Foundation Trust</strong> in London, where he contributed to ovarian tissue cryopreservation research and cleanroom quality assurance, and the <strong>Limassol Clinic IVF Lab</strong> in Cyprus, where he currently serves as Clinical Embryologist. He is experienced in laboratory quality control, regulatory compliance (CMAR, cGMP, HTA), and the continuous improvement of standard operating procedures.</p>
